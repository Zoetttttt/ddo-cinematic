## Updated experience

- One animation clock connects the damped DDO spring, a larger brand-blue DDO entrance on composited vector layers, with successive restrained rebounds flowing directly into the original third O travelling intact to the centre, small dot, circular reveal of the first model, centred model-to-model transitions with restrained shared squash and release, and the final frame morphing into the actual header logo. The letter stage avoids full-screen canvas redraws and has no staged pause before the O moves. The approved model sequence, logo handover, homepage, cursor, fonts and Services layout are retained. All thirteen supplied frames are decoded before playback. Each model inherits a small, shared deformation from the preceding frame and settles at the same centre and normalized size. Two reusable full-frame canvases preserve a stable white background during blending. The final model hold clamps the previous-frame blend so earlier models cannot flash back during the hold. The automatic handover starts against the same opaque white background, avoiding a one-frame brightness seam. Cropping is performed by the renderer using source bounds; the image files are retained, presentation borders are excluded and the model artwork is centred in a common display envelope. No artwork is redrawn. Click anywhere, Enter, Space or Escape dissolves the opening into geometric fragments; there is no visible skip button. The opening runs once per tab and the small homepage dot replays it. Reduced-motion users enter directly.
- Geometric Jost typography chosen to follow the monoline circular forms of the supplied logo wordmark. Jost is not claimed to be a verified identification of the original logo font. Its open font licence is included.
- Five homepage scenes in order: Sumi Bar, Arctic White, KOKO / More Fun video, Dragon Hot Pot, Yebisu. The supplied video was already 3838 × 2158; this original resolution is preserved in the desktop version, with a 1920-wide version for mobile. Standard colour conversion and high-quality web encoding are used; no generative detail was added.
- The homepage uses the original cinematic edition’s 3.2-second photo dissolves, 32-second camera drift and 16-second still-image timing, with the requested five-scene order and More Fun video. The background does not move with the pointer. Existing Jost typography and music are retained. The system cursor is untouched. Only clickable text has a slight elastic hover scale; there is no custom cursor or cursor movement code. Navigation indicators remain centred under the text labels.
- The bilingual Services page has DDO’s own asymmetric masthead, orbital brand detail, two-by-two open service fields, offset panoramic photograph and editorial dialogue. It retains the requested clarity and spacing while moving away from the reference page’s centred title, four-column structure and diptych.
- Purple Projects accent; Food & Beverage and Immersive Space category names.
- Red Studio accent; the large D-shaped mark has no inner letter. Six team portraits and the supplied introduction are included.
- New photography comes from the supplied Dropbox banner originals and print-quality portfolio. The full supplied team JPEG is retained at `assets/team-story-original.jpg`; portrait views are cropped for the page without redrawing their content.
